Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026 · Applies to the Rokko app for iPad

In short: Rokko does not collect, send or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no internet connection. Everything the app saves stays on your iPad.

What Rokko stores, and where

To work, Rokko saves a small amount of information only on your iPad, inside the app's own storage:

  • Your settings: Calm or Standard look, and sound on or off.
  • Lesson progress: which step you reached, and an estimate of how well each chess piece is known.
  • A record of answers in the games (for example, which piece was tapped, whether a hint was used and how long it took), used to choose the next game and to show the grown-up's summary at the end of the lesson.
  • Break and mood check-ins chosen by the grown-up.

Rokko does not ask for a name, age, email address, photo, location or any other personal details. There is one child profile per iPad, and it has no name.

What Rokko does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet. The app contains no network code.
  • It does not send any data to us or to anyone else.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party code (SDKs).
  • It does not use iCloud or sync between devices.
  • It does not export or share data.
  • It does not use the camera, microphone, contacts or location.

Children's privacy

Rokko is made for children to use with a parent or carer. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, no personal information from children is collected, used or disclosed. This is how we meet the requirements of children's privacy laws such as COPPA in the United States and the GDPR in the European Union and the UK.

Deleting your data

All of Rokko's data lives on your iPad. To delete it, delete the Rokko app. Its settings and progress are removed with it. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If Rokko ever started collecting data, we would update this policy before releasing that version.
