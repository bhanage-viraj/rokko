Chess, taught the way your child learns.

Rokko teaches chess to autistic children, aged 6 to 10, through stories, hands-on games and a real chessboard, with a grown-up beside them. It adapts to each child as they play.

Many autistic children learn best by seeing, touching and doing.

So Rokko doesn't teach chess with rules on a screen. Every lesson moves through three kinds of learning, one after another.

📖

A story to see

A short film gives every chess piece a character and a reason. Pictures and a story come first; the names and rules hang on to them.

👆

Games to touch

Small games your child taps and drags: find the piece, match it, build a team. One thing on screen at a time, with spoken help.

♟️

A board to hold

Between games, the grown-up hands over the real piece. Your child holds it, points to it and sets it on a real chessboard.

It adapts to your child

No two children learn the same way, so Rokko watches how each game goes and quietly adjusts.

🌱

Their pace

Games get a little harder only when your child is ready, one small step at a time, and only between games, never in the middle.

🤝

Help that fades

If your child is stuck, a clue appears, then a pointer, then the answer. Help shrinks as they grow more sure.

🌤️

Their day

Having a wobbly day? The grown-up says so, and Rokko eases off: simpler games, calmer pictures, or a break.

How a lesson goes

  1. Watch the story togetherA short film introduces the chess pieces. Captions show every word.
  2. Meet each pieceThe story shows what each piece is. Then the grown-up holds up the real one from your board.
  3. Play little gamesTap and drag to find, match and sort the pieces. The games follow your child's pace.
  4. Play together at the real boardThe lesson ends where it belongs: the two of you and a real chessboard.

Calm by design

🌿

Nothing rushes

Every step waits for the grown-up to tap Next. Nothing moves on by itself.

🍃

Calm mode

Softer pictures, gentle fades and quieter sound. It also follows the iPad's Reduce Motion setting.

💬

Kind feedback

No scores, no buzzers, no red. A try that doesn't fit gently sinks back so your child can try again.

👥

Grown-up in charge

Pause any time, take a break, go back or skip ahead with the lesson path.

🎧

Every line spoken

All lines are recorded voices, with captions that match word for word.

🧩

Predictable

Buttons never move, and every question is asked the same way, so your child always knows what comes next.

🔒

Nothing leaves your iPad

Rokko has no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no internet connection at all. Your child's progress is kept only on your iPad.
