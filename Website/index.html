Six animals. Six chess pieces.

Lesson 1, The Day the Jungle Changed, opens with a short film. Then each animal turns into its piece, and you hold up the real one.

How it works

  1. Watch the story togetherA short film meets the animals of the Checkerwood Jungle. Captions show every word.
  2. Meet each pieceAn animal becomes its chess piece, then the grown-up holds up the real one from your board.
  3. Play little gamesFind the piece, match it, set up a team. The games get a little harder only when your child is ready.
  4. Play together at the real boardThe lesson ends where it belongs: the two of you and a real chessboard.

Calm by design

🌿

Nothing rushes

Every step waits for the grown-up to tap Next. Nothing moves on by itself.

🍃

Calm mode

Softer pictures, gentle fades and quieter sound. It also follows the iPad's Reduce Motion setting.

💬

Kind feedback

No scores, no buzzers, no red. A try that doesn't fit gently sinks back so your child can try again.

👥

Grown-up in charge

Pause any time, take a break, go back or skip ahead with the lesson path.

🎧

Every line spoken

All lines are recorded voices, with captions that match word for word.

♟️

A real board

Built to be used next to a real chess set, not instead of one.

🔒

Nothing leaves your iPad

Rokko has no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no internet connection at all. Your child's progress is kept only on your iPad.
