Common questions

How do we get Rokko?

Rokko is in beta. Install TestFlight on your iPad, then open the Rokko beta invite on that iPad and tap Accept. You can also send feedback from inside TestFlight.

What do we need?

An iPad and a real chess set. Rokko is made to be used side by side, with the board next to you.

How do we move on?

Tap Next at the bottom right. Nothing moves on by itself. Tap ↻ next to Next to watch a clip again.

Can we go back or skip ahead?

Yes. Use the lesson path at the top of the screen: ‹ and › move one step, or tap any symbol to jump to that part.

How do we pause or take a break?

Tap the pause button at the top left. To check in on how your child is feeling, hold the Guardian button (top right) for two seconds.

The pictures move too much for us.

Open Settings (the gear on the lessons screen) and choose Calm. Rokko also follows the iPad's Reduce Motion setting.

Can we turn the sound off?

Yes, in Settings. Captions stay on, so every line can still be read.

How do we start again from the beginning?

Delete and reinstall the app. This removes all progress, which is only stored on your iPad.

Is our data shared?

No. Rokko has no internet connection and stores everything only on your iPad. See the privacy policy.
